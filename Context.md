# RELINK — Project Summary

## Problem Statement

During natural disasters such as floods, cyclones, and landslides, affected communities often struggle to access food, drinking water, medicines, and other essential supplies. Relief organizations, NGOs, government agencies, and volunteers face difficulties coordinating their efforts, identifying areas with the greatest needs, tracking available resources, and ensuring efficient last-mile delivery. These problems become more severe when mobile networks, internet connectivity, and other communication infrastructure are disrupted. Consequently, relief supplies may be delayed, unevenly distributed, or fail to reach isolated communities in time.

## Proposed Solution

RELINK is a disaster-response and relief-coordination concept designed to improve the distribution of food and essential supplies during emergencies, especially in areas with limited or disrupted connectivity.

The project aims to connect affected communities, relief organizations, volunteers, and supply providers through a coordinated system for communicating needs, identifying available resources, and organizing relief distribution.

RELINK explores technologies such as satellite communication, mesh networking, Radio over IP (RoIP), SMS/USSD, and LoRaWAN to support communication under different infrastructure conditions. These technologies could help maintain communication between responders, share information about resource availability and local needs, and coordinate the movement of essential supplies when conventional communication networks are unavailable or unreliable.

## Core Objectives

1. Improve coordination between disaster-response organizations and volunteers.
2. Identify where food and essential supplies are needed most.
3. Improve visibility into available resources and outstanding requests.
4. Support communication in areas where conventional networks are disrupted.
5. Reduce duplicated relief efforts and improve last-mile distribution.
6. Help ensure that vulnerable and isolated communities receive timely assistance.

## Target Users

- Disaster-affected individuals and families
- NGOs and humanitarian organizations
- Government disaster-management agencies
- Relief volunteers and community coordinators
- Food and essential-supply providers
- Emergency-response teams

## Technology Areas

- Networking and communication systems
- Satellite communication
- Mesh networking
- Radio over IP (RoIP)
- SMS and USSD
- LoRaWAN and low-power communication
- Resource allocation and logistics coordination
- Software systems for tracking requests and supplies

## Intended Impact

RELINK aims to make disaster relief more coordinated, resilient, and equitable by improving communication between stakeholders and helping essential supplies reach affected communities efficiently.

The concept is particularly relevant to regions vulnerable to floods, cyclones, and other natural disasters, where damaged infrastructure and logistical challenges can complicate emergency response.

## Development Consideration

RELINK is a proposed concept. Its specific architecture, communication mechanisms, and operational feasibility would need to be validated through prototyping and testing. The project should begin with a clearly defined use case, such as coordinating food requests and available supplies in an area experiencing disrupted connectivity, before expanding to more complex disaster-response capabilities.
